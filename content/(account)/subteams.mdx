A subteam is a team inside your team. It has its own name, URL, members, and Spaces. It uses the parent team's plan, seats, and billing.

Subteams need an Enterprise parent team. On every other plan, subteam operations return `enterprise_required`. Plans are on [Plans and billing](/billing).

## How subteams work

- **The parent's owners and admins are in every subteam.** They hold the same role there. To change that role, change it on the parent team.
- **Parent members can join a subteam.** A subteam appears in **Teams you can join** for parent members. Pressing **Join** makes them a subteam `member`, with no new seat. A person removed from a subteam does not see it there. An owner or admin of the parent team must add them again.
- **Seats are shared.** Seats count across the parent and all its subteams. A person in several teams of the family uses one seat.
- **Subteams do not nest.** A subteam cannot have subteams of its own.
- **Leaving the parent leaves every subteam.** When someone leaves or is removed from the parent team, they also lose every subteam membership.

Each subteam owns its Spaces. Access to them follows subteam membership and the member's subteam role. Teams, roles, and Space ownership work as described in [Teams and members](/teams).

## Create a subteam

Only owners and admins of the parent team can create a subteam.

1. Open the parent team and go to **Subteams**.
2. Press **New subteam**.
3. Enter a **Name**, then press **Create subteam**.

Spacefast creates the subteam and opens it. Its URL slug starts with the parent's slug, followed by the name.

Over HTTP, send [`createSubTeam`](/api/reference/teams/createsubteam) with a `name` of 1 to 255 characters:

```bash
curl "https://api.spacefast.com/v1/teams/$TEAM_ID/subteams" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $SPACEFAST_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"name":"Design"}'
```

The `201` response holds the subteam: `id`, `teamId` (the parent), `name`, `slug`, `memberCount`, `createdAt`, and `updatedAt`. From a terminal, run `sf teams subteams create Design`.

## List subteams

[`listSubTeams`](/api/reference/teams/listsubteams) returns the parent's subteams, sorted by name. Every member of the parent team can list them. From a terminal, run `sf teams subteams ls`.

The **Subteams** page shows the subteams you are in, each with **Open**. Below them are the subteams you can join, each with **Join**.

## Rename a subteam

Send [`updateSubTeam`](/api/reference/teams/updatesubteam) with a new `name`. The slug does not change. From a terminal:

```bash
sf teams subteams rename "$SUBTEAM_ID" "Brand design"
```

You can also open the subteam and rename it in **Team settings → General**.

## Manage subteam members

Adding and removing members needs an owner or admin of the parent team.

```bash
sf teams subteams members ls "$SUBTEAM_ID"
sf teams subteams members add "$SUBTEAM_ID" "$USER_ID"
sf teams subteams members rm "$SUBTEAM_ID" "$USER_ID"
```

| Operation | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [`listSubTeamMembers`](/api/reference/teams/listsubteammembers) | Lists members with their `userId`, `name`, `email`, and `image`. Subteam members and the parent's owners and admins can read it |
| [`addSubTeamMember`](/api/reference/teams/addsubteammember) | Adds a parent member as a subteam `member`. No invitation is sent and no seat is used. Repeating the request keeps one membership |
| [`removeSubTeamMember`](/api/reference/teams/removesubteammember) | Removes the person from the subteam and its Spaces. They stay on the parent team |

Find a user ID with `sf teams members ls --json` on the parent team. You can also invite someone from the **Members** page of the subteam. The subteam role picker offers **Admin** and **Member**. A subteam has no owner of its own.

A person you remove from a subteam cannot rejoin it through **Teams you can join**. To bring them back, add them again.

## Delete a subteam

A subteam with Spaces cannot be deleted. Delete or move its Spaces first. See [Move a Space to another team](/teams#move-a-space-to-another-team).

Send [`deleteSubTeam`](/api/reference/teams/deletesubteam), or run `sf teams subteams rm <subteam-id>`. In the dashboard, open the subteam and go to **Team settings → Danger zone → Delete this team**. Deleting a subteam ends its memberships.

A parent team cannot be deleted while it has subteams. Delete every subteam first.

## Errors

| Code | Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `enterprise_required` | 403 | The parent team is not on Enterprise |
| `parent_authority_required` | 403 | Only an owner or admin of the parent team can do this |
| `subteam_nesting_unsupported` | 422 | The team is already a subteam, or it is a system or partner team |
| `role_managed_by_parent` | 409 | The person's subteam role comes from the parent team. Change it there |
| `team_has_spaces` | 409 | The subteam still has Spaces |
| `team_has_subteams` | 409 | The parent team still has subteams |
| `not_found` | 404 | The subteam does not exist, or the user is not a member of the parent team |
| `forbidden` | 403 | Your role cannot manage members, or you are not in the subteam whose members you asked for |
