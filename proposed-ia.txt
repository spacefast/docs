SPACEFAST DOCUMENTATION — IMPLEMENTED INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

The documentation starts with what a person is trying to do. Product and
protocol names appear one level later, where they help rather than lead.

The paths above ROUTE POLICY are the required public navigation. The route
verification script checks that every path appears in the sidebar and produces
an indexable page in the built site.

ROUTE POLICY

Authored pages move directly to the paths above. There are no compatibility
redirects because the site has not launched.

Generated routes stay at /api/reference, /platforms/api/reference, /cli,
/errors, and /changelog. Those paths are embedded in generated artifacts and
tool output, so the navigation groups them under Reference without renaming
their public contracts.
