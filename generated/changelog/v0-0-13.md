## @spacefast/common

### Patch Changes

- Reject the reserved `x-spacefast-*` / `x-stattic-*` response-header namespaces when compiling `_headers`, instead of failing later at publish. The platform-managed header policy now has one definition (`@spacefast/common` static-runtime-policy) that the `_headers` compiler consumes directly.
