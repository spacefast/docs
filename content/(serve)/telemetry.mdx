Telemetry shows who's on your Space now and how visits change over time. Use **Now** to watch live activity and **Trends** to explore visitor history over 7, 30, or 90 days.

## Open Telemetry

Open your Space in the dashboard and choose **Telemetry**. You must be signed in as an editor of the Space.

Telemetry is in beta and must be enabled for both your team and your Space. If the menu is missing, or the page says **Analytics is off for this Space**, contact [support](mailto:support@spacefast.com). There is no self-service switch to enable the beta.

The dashboard page does not require Comments. The live and history views depend on separate services: if only one is configured, only its card appears. **Telemetry isn't set up here** means neither is available on that deployment.

Collection on published pages requires **Page enhancements** to be on in the Space's **Settings**. Telemetry uses a browser tracker, unlike the script-free edge stats. Opening the dashboard does not by itself start collecting visits from a site with page enhancements off.

## What can I do with Telemetry?

| View | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| Now | See how many visitors are here, which pages they're on, and recent arrivals |
| Now | Inspect sources, countries, engagement, and recent events where those measurements are available |
| Trends | Follow visitors, page views, and engaged time over 7, 30, or 90 days |
| Trends | Find top pages, compare page traffic with the previous period, and inspect engagement by page |
| Trends | Review referrers and scroll depth |

The Space's analytics profile changes which live views come first. A measurement the source cannot supply is shown as unavailable or left out, not counted as zero. Visitor analytics counts visitors without listing individual people.

If Trends cannot be reached, reload the page to try again. A gap or an error is not evidence that nobody visited.

## Watch on the published site

For a published Space, the page can offer **Watch on site** or **Open site**. **Watch on site** opens the live site with its Telemetry drawer. That drawer requires page enhancements and Comments enabled on the live Space, not only on previews. Otherwise, **Open site** opens the site without promising a drawer.

You can use the dashboard's Now and Trends cards without enabling Comments.

## Telemetry or traffic stats?

Telemetry is visitor analytics. [Traffic stats](/stats) are the edge's request accounting, available under **Settings → Stats** and through `sf analytics`. They count requests and views differently and use windows of 48 hours, 7 days, or 30 days. The CLI command does not return the Telemetry cards.

Use Stats to inspect requests, bandwidth, and busy paths. Use Telemetry to watch visitors and explore engagement over time. Do not expect their totals to match.

[Google Analytics and Tag Manager](/customization) are separate integrations. Adding one of those tags does not enable the Telemetry beta.
