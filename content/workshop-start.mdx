Connect Spacefast to the AI tool you already use, then make and share one small page.

## Connect your AI tool

Open the setup guide for your tool and follow it through authorization. Keep your email inbox available if Spacefast asks you to sign in.

| Your tool | Setup guide | Before connecting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ChatGPT | [Connect ChatGPT](https://my.spacefast.com/start/chatgpt) | Use ChatGPT on the web. Plugin availability and custom MCP connections depend on your account and workspace security settings. |
| Claude | [Connect Claude](https://my.spacefast.com/start/claude-app) | Sign in to Claude. On a Team or Enterprise plan, an owner must add the connector first. |
| Codex | [Connect Codex](https://my.spacefast.com/start/codex) | Use a Codex session where you can install the plugin or configure an MCP server. |

## Choose and copy a recipe

In your access email, click **Build your first recipe**. You can also [open the recipe page](https://my.spacefast.com/first-recipe) directly. Choose a checklist, calculator, or dashboard, then copy its prompt.

Paste the prompt into the same conversation where you connected Spacefast. Answer any questions, then review the page and ask for changes in that conversation. The recipe starts with one static `index.html` and fictional data.

The recipe checks your connected account before publishing. If it needs you to choose a team, it asks. If it cannot reach your account, it stops and explains how to reconnect.

## Open and share the result

When publishing finishes, the agent returns a dashboard link and a viewer link for the same Space. Open the viewer link and check the page. Test the link in a private browser window where you are signed out, or ask someone else to open it.

If the agent does not return a viewer link, open the Space in [your dashboard](https://my.spacefast.com), choose **Access → Create share link → Can view**, and test that link while signed out. See [Sharing and access](/access) for other access options.

To make a change, return to the same AI conversation and ask the agent to update the existing Space. Open the viewer link again to check the result.

Publishing through your connected account saves the Space there; it does not require a claim step. If you choose [Drop](https://my.spacefast.com/drop) and publish without signing in, see [Publish without an account](/anonymous-and-claim) for the claim link and expiry details.

## If you get stuck

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Spacefast is missing or cannot reach your account | Reopen the setup guide, confirm installation and authorization, and enable Spacefast in the conversation if your tool asks. Check workspace policy if the connection is blocked. |
| Publishing failed | Record the visible error and job or request ID, check the publish status, then use [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting). Do not create another Space solely to retry an uncertain publish. |
| The viewer link says private or returns 403 | In the intended Space, create a **Can view** share link under **Access**. Test that link while signed out. |
| You published through Drop without signing in | Keep the private claim link and follow [Publish without an account](/anonymous-and-claim) before its displayed deadline. |
