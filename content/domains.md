---
title: Custom domains
description: Documentation for custom domains, DNS setup, SSL, primary domains, and domain diagnostics.
---

Bring your own domain to serve a space from a hostname you control.

1. Add the domain in your space settings.
2. Follow the DNS values and diagnostics shown by Spacefast for that domain.

SSL is set up automatically once DNS resolves. There are no certificates to create, upload, or renew.

## Plan limits

| Plan     | External domains |
| -------- | ---------------- |
| Free     | 3                |
| Personal | 10               |
| Work     | Unlimited        |

One registered domain counts once, including all of its subdomains: `example.com`, `www.example.com`, and `docs.example.com` are one domain, not three. Domains bought through Spacefast never count against these limits.

Most limits warn before they block, and limits only move up.

Anonymous spaces cannot use custom domains. Claim the space first.
