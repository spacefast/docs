Spacefast for Platforms is for companies that sell infrastructure as part of their own product: web hosts, domain registrars, agencies, and organizations that run many sites. Spacefast runs the infrastructure. Your customers see your brand, your pricing, and your dashboard.

Spacefast for Platforms is part of WP Cloud, the infrastructure web hosts use to power their own WordPress products. It runs on the same infrastructure as Automattic, WP Cloud, Pressable, and WordPress.com. The [Partner API](/platforms/partner-api) extends it to the sites and apps your customers build with agents.

## Who it's for

| You are | What Spacefast for Platforms gives you |
| --- | --- |
| A web host | Managed publishing and infrastructure for your customers, without running servers or a control panel |
| A domain registrar | A live site at the point of registration, with no infrastructure to build |
| An agency or reseller | One integration for every client site, billed and branded as your own |
| An organization with many sites | Sites provisioned from code for each team, with one account over all of them |

## What you control

- **Your brand.** Set your name, logo, support links, and error docs once. Claims and access handoffs land on your own portal.
- **Your customers.** Each customer is an ID from your own system. Spacefast creates them on first use, with no separate signup.
- **Your pricing.** Declare plans with their own features and quotas. Read usage records per customer and bill however you want.
- **Your domains.** Serve customer sites from your own hostname, and put the API behind your own origin.
- **Your UX.** Everything is API-first. Build your own interface, or give customers scoped tokens and let their apps call Spacefast directly.

## What Spacefast runs

Publishing, builds, versions and rollbacks, custom domains and certificates, and CDN and caching. Customer sites get the same serving path as every other Space.

## How integration works

1. **Get partner access.** Your account gets a live tenant and a linked test tenant. The test tenant simulates provisioning and is metered at zero.
2. **Create customer Spaces.** Publish for a customer with a tenant API key and their ID. See the [Partner API quickstart](/platforms/partner-api).
3. **Configure your account.** Branding, signing keys, plans, event subscriptions, and hostnames live in one [system Space](/platforms/partner-api/configuration).
4. **Hand customers access.** Issue [customer JWTs](/platforms/partner-api/tokens) scoped to one customer.
5. **Go live.** Exercise failures in test mode, then [promote your configuration](/platforms/partner-api/go-live) to the live tenant.
