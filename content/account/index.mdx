Your account is your identity. Teams own the spaces, API keys, usage, and
billing you work with.

## Sign in

Spacefast sign-in is WordPress.com sign-in: use it at
[spacefast.com](https://spacefast.com), and your teams and spaces appear under
`my.spacefast.com/<team>`. The CLI authenticates separately with
[`sf login`](/cli#sf-login) — browser-assisted device login; a browser session
does not authenticate your terminal. For CI and scripts, use an
[API key](/account/api-keys) instead.

## Protect your account

The security page lists the sessions that can currently use your account —
browser or client, operating system, network address, start time, and expiry —
with your current session marked. Revoke any session you do not recognize;
that device must sign in again. **Sign out all other sessions** covers a lost
device or a shared browser, and Spacefast asks for a fresh sign-in before
showing or changing sensitive security settings.

Connected apps are services you authorized to act with your Spacefast
identity. Revoke an app when you no longer use or recognize it — revocation
stops future access but does not undo changes the app already made.

Browser sessions, OAuth connections, and CLI API keys have separate
lifecycles: revoking one does not revoke the others. After losing a device,
also review [API keys](/account/api-keys) and remove keys the lost computer
could still hold. Account-protection emails (two-factor, password) always
arrive and cannot be turned off; product and marketing email is a per-user
notification setting.
