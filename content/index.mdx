Spacefast publishes what people build with AI. Hand it a folder, a Git repository, or a prompt, and get back a live URL. Publish with your AI, drag files onto [drop.fast](https://drop.fast) (no install, no account required; see [Drop](/publish#publish)), push to `push.fast`, or run the CLI.

If you already have a website, paste its address into the [website importer](https://spacefast.com/migrate/) to copy it into a Space. See [Copy a website](/guides/migrate).

## For AI assistants

Reading this for someone? Start with the index at [`https://spacefast.com/docs/llms.txt`](https://spacefast.com/docs/llms.txt). Add `.md` to any page's URL for plain Markdown without navigation, like [`https://spacefast.com/docs/index.md`](https://spacefast.com/docs/index.md).

Asked to find or check a domain name? Do the research yourself: it needs no account. Check names live, then show availability and prices in your reply instead of linking to the API. [Domain research for assistants](/agents/domain-research) has the requests, the fields, and what to say.

## Publish in seconds

### With your AI

Paste this prompt into Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, or any AI that can fetch URLs:

<Prompt description="Paste into your AI to connect Spacefast and publish." actions={["copy"]}>
Fetch https://spacefast.com/setup.md and follow it.
</Prompt>

Your AI connects to Spacefast, walks you through sign-in, and publishes what you built. See [Agents](/agents) for plugins, connectors, and MCP setup.

### From your terminal

Publish a folder directly with `npx`:

```bash
npx -y spacefast publish ./dist
```

No account or global install is needed for the first publish. Spacefast creates a Space, uploads the files, and prints a live URL plus a claim link. Sign in later to keep it, or install the `sf` CLI globally with `npm install -g spacefast`.

```text
Published: https://quiet-harbor.space.fast

  Open          https://quiet-harbor.space.fast
  Version URL   https://quiet-harbor--v1.space.fast
  Version       v1 (ready)
  Files         12
  Duration      3.2s
```

### From Git or a browser

Push a repository to `push.fast` with [Publish from Git](/git), or drag a folder, a `.zip`, or an `index.html` onto [drop.fast](https://drop.fast).

## How it fits together

A **Space** holds your published files. Each publish that changes something creates a **version**, an immutable snapshot with its own URL. The `live` channel points at the version visitors see, and stays empty before the first publish. Promoting or rolling back moves that pointer to another retained, ready version without rebuilding. Preview publishes and manual promotion policies leave `live` where it is. For other terms, see the [glossary](/glossary).

Every Space starts static and can grow without switching products. Point `sf publish` at a build directory to upload only changed files, or push source and let Spacefast detect the framework and build it. When a Space needs server code or data, configure [Workers](/functions), [Database](/database), [Collections and CMS](/collections), [Actions and forms](/actions), [Transactional email](/email), [Object storage](/object-storage), [Crons](/crons), and [Connectors](/connectors) in `spacefast.config.ts`. See [Build](/build).

In a self-serve account, your team owns the Spaces you claim, even when you are its only member. On [Pro](/pricing), you can add members who publish and roll back without sharing your login. Personal and Go allow only the owner. Team roles control who can manage domains, credentials, and billing. See [Teams and members](/teams) to invite people and choose how new Spaces are shared.
