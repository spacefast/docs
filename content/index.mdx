Spacefast turns static files and app builds into live URLs. Publish with
your coding agent, one CLI command, or a drag onto
[drop.website](https://drop.website) — before you even have an account —
then claim the space to keep it. Every publish creates an immutable version,
and rolling back is a pointer move, not a rebuild.
