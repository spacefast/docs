## Billing questions

### How am I billed?

Monthly, in US dollars, before tax. Plans and Boost bill at the start of the month; usage past what's included bills at the end. Manage it in [Plans and billing](/billing).

### What if I upgrade or downgrade?

Upgrades apply right away, prorated to the day. Downgrades apply at renewal; anything over the new limits keeps serving but becomes read-only until it's back under.

### Can prices change?

We may change our fees going forward, in line with the [Terms of Service](https://wordpress.com/tos/). If the price of a plan you already pay for goes up, you keep your current price for at least 12 months and get at least 60 days' notice. If you don't agree, you can cancel. Price cuts apply to everyone.

### Can I cap my spending?

Yes, on Go, Pro and Business. The cap starts at twice your plan price, with alerts at 50%, 75% and 100%. You're never charged above it, even if usage briefly runs past it.

### Do you have nonprofit or education pricing?

Yes. Contact sales.
