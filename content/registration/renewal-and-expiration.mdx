A registration lasts one year. To keep the domain, renew it before it expires. Auto-renew is on by default and does this for you.

## Auto-renew

As long as **Auto-renew** is on, the domain renews itself for another year before it expires. Here's the timeline:

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 60 days before expiry | The team's owners and admins get an email and a notification: the domain renews on this date, for this price. |
| 30 days before expiry | Spacefast charges the team's saved payment method and adds a year to the registration. |
| If the charge fails | Owners and admins are told what went wrong (declined card, no card, bank verification needed), with a link to fix it. Spacefast tries again on its own, several times, until the expiry date. |
| On the expiry date | Nothing more is tried. The domain [expires](#when-a-domain-expires). |

If you buy a domain, or turn auto-renew on, less than 37 days before it expires, the charge waits at least a week after the notice. The **Registration** card shows the exact date with "Auto-renew charges the saved payment method from…".

The renewal price is the extension's registration price on the day it renews, in US dollars plus tax. It may differ from what you paid the first time.

We strongly recommend leaving auto-renew on. Once a domain expires, getting it back can be hard, and someone else may register it first.

### Turn auto-renew off or on

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Dashboard">

Open your team's **Domains**, click the domain, and flip the **Auto-renew** switch on the **Registration** card. Only team owners and admins can change it.

</Tab>
<Tab title="API">

`PATCH /v1/domains/{domainId}/registration` with `{"autoRenew": false}`. The registration's `autoRenewChargesFrom` gives the earliest charge date, or `null` when auto-renew can't renew this term.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

Turning auto-renew off cancels any automatic renewal that hasn't been charged yet.

## Renew by hand

You can renew a domain yourself at any time before it's gone. The dashboard shows the button from 30 days before expiry; the CLI renews whenever you ask.

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Dashboard">

Open the domain and click **Renew for 1 year** on the **Registration** card. You go to checkout to pay.

</Tab>
<Tab title="CLI">

```bash
sf domains registration renew yourgroovydomain.com
```

This prints a checkout link. Nothing is charged until someone pays there.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

A renewal adds one year to the current expiry date, so renewing early never costs you days. After paying, the page says "Paid. Renewing now; the new expiry shows up in a minute."

If a renewal can't go through at the registry, it's refunded automatically and the **Registration** card says so.

## When a domain expires

Spacefast sends no renewal charge after the expiry date. The domain's status turns to **Expired**, and the card says "Expired on [date]. Renew it soon, before someone else can register it."

### The redemption window

Spacefast keeps an expired domain renewable for about **30 days** after it expires. During that window:

- **Your Space keeps serving** on the domain as long as the registry keeps it in the DNS. Some registries park or stop resolving an expired name sooner. Your site content is never deleted.
- **You can renew** with **Renew for 1 year** or `sf domains registration renew`, the same way as before it expired.

The exact grace and redemption rules come from the registry for each extension. A name the registry has already moved into its own redemption period may not renew through Spacefast. If the renewal is refused, your payment is refunded.

### After the window

If the domain isn't renewed, Spacefast checks with the registrar once the window ends:

- If the registration was renewed or restored at the registrar, Spacefast picks up the new expiry date.
- If the registry released the name, the registration is marked as deleted and Spacefast DNS stops answering for it. The domain stays in your team's list as a regular, unregistered domain.

Once a name goes back on the market, anyone can register it, often within seconds. Back-order services wait for exactly that. Never let a domain you care about expire.

## Check when a domain expires

Open your team's **Domains**. Each registered domain shows "Expires [date]", and you can sort by **Expires soonest**. Badges flag **Renewal due** and **Expired**.

From the terminal:

```bash
sf domains registration yourgroovydomain.com
```

## Errors you might see

| Code | What happened | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`domain_registration_not_renewable`](/errors/domain_registration_not_renewable) | The registration isn't active or expired (it's still registering, failed, or was released), so there's nothing to renew. | Check the registration with `sf domains registration`. |
| `purchase_pending` | A renewal for this domain is already waiting to be paid. | Finish that one from the **Registration** card. |
| `purchase_in_progress` | The domain is paid for, and its renewal or refund is still going through. | Wait a minute and check again. |
| [`registration_expiring`](/errors/registration_expiring) | Diagnostic: the registration expires soon. | Renew it, or make sure auto-renew is on with a working payment method. |
| [`registration_expired`](/errors/registration_expired) | Diagnostic: the registration has expired. | Renew it before the redemption window ends. |
