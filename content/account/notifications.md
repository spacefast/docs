---
title: Choose email notifications
description: Pick which optional Spacefast emails you want to receive.
---

You can choose email for:

- product and service updates;
- team invitations;
- announcements;
- tips and offers.

Change these choices from your account notification settings. The change
applies to your user account, not to every member of a team.

Essential security messages still arrive. Two-factor, password, and other
account-protection emails cannot be turned off with marketing or product-email
preferences.
