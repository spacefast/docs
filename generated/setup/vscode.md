**[Add to VS Code](https://vscode.dev/redirect/mcp/install?name=spacefast&config=%7B%22type%22%3A%22http%22%2C%22url%22%3A%22https%3A%2F%2Fmcp.spacefast.com%22%7D)**

```text
https://mcp.spacefast.com
```
