Long-running work should not turn into a mystery spinner. The dashboard groups
it in the **Work** panel.

## Read the Work panel

Work is sorted into five useful buckets:

- **Doing now** — work currently running.
- **Needs you** — a decision, approval, or missing input is blocking progress.
- **Waiting** — accepted work that has not started or is waiting on another step.
- **Saved work** — work you kept for later.
- **Recent** — completed and failed work with its final result.

Open an item for its current step, timestamps, result, and available actions.
Domains, variables, webhooks, integrations, and agent approvals can all appear
here when they continue beyond the page where you started them.

## During a browser upload

Browser uploads move through **Preparing**, **Uploading**, **Finalizing**, and
**Publishing**. Keep the tab open until the upload has been handed off. After
that, the server owns the remaining work and it is safe to leave.

The existing live version stays live until the new publish succeeds. A failed
publish does not replace it.

For an audit trail after work finishes, use the [activity feed](/spaces/activity).
