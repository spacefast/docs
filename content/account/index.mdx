This page covers signing in and keeping your account secure. The
[teams](/account/teams) you belong to own the spaces,
[API keys](/account/api-keys), usage, and billing you work with.

## Sign in

Spacefast sign-in is WordPress.com sign-in: use it at
[spacefast.com](https://spacefast.com), and your teams and spaces appear
under `my.spacefast.com/<team>`. The CLI authenticates separately with
[`sf login`](/cli#sf-login) — a browser session does not authenticate your
terminal. For CI and scripts, use an [API key](/account/api-keys) instead.

## Protect your account

The security page lists the sessions that can currently use your account,
with your current session marked. Revoke any session you do not recognize,
and that device must sign in again; **Sign out all other sessions** covers
a lost device or a shared browser.

Connected apps are services you authorized to act with your Spacefast
identity. Revoke an app when you no longer use or recognize it. Revocation
stops future access but does not undo changes the app already made.

Browser sessions, OAuth connections, and CLI API keys have separate
lifecycles — revoking one does not revoke the others. After losing a
device, also review [API keys](/account/api-keys) and remove keys the lost
computer could still hold.

Product and marketing email is a per-user notification setting.

## Two-factor authentication and device approvals

Enroll a second factor on the account security page. CLI and agent
sign-ins use device authorization: `sf login` shows a code, and the browser
asks you to approve the device. Approve only sign-ins you started; if a
code appears that you did not request, deny it.

## Notifications

The dashboard has a notification inbox behind the bell in the top bar.
Email notification preferences and unsubscribe controls live on your
account.

## Multiple accounts in the CLI

`sf profiles` lists the CLI's configured provider profiles, and the global
`--profile` flag picks one per command, so one machine can work against
several accounts.
