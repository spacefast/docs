Free has fixed allowances. Go and Plus use shared monthly credit: {{go-credit}} on Go and {{plus-credit}} on Plus. Usage above the credit is billed monthly at the same rates on both plans.

## Free allowances

These are per team each month, except storage, which is a retained-byte allowance, and email, which has a daily limit. Free is for personal and non-commercial use.

<include>/_pricing/usage.md</include>

The CPU allowance combines PHP, Zero and SQL. Each other allowance stands on its own; unused build minutes cannot buy more transfer. Free has no dollar credit and no automatic overage bill.

Exceeding an allowance alerts Spacefast for review. A Space may be suspended until the applicable allowance resets, retained storage falls below its limit, or the team upgrades. Data stays and exports remain available.

## Go and Plus rates

Prices are USD before tax. The included quantities below belong to the whole team, not to each Space. Remaining charges draw from one shared monthly credit.

CPU means active processing time, excluding waits. There is no memory charge. Build minutes measure elapsed machine time, including installation and idle time; platform-caused retries are not billed.

An invocation is one PHP or Zero origin execution, including a request that bypasses the cache or cron run. Cached hits and Worker requests are excluded. Workers have their own request meter.

Cached delivery counts cached response bytes. Non-cached transfer counts origin misses, bypasses and PHP or Zero response bytes. Worker responses are excluded from that transfer charge. Bytes are counted once on the path that produced them.

Email counts recipient deliveries, so one message to three people counts as three. Daily delivery safeguards still apply. A credit balance does not override them; check the current entitlement before sending.

## Storage classes

Files and media include app-user uploads, media, source and Git history, non-live versions, and customer-saved backup archives. Their paid rate appears in the usage table above. Storage uses time-weighted logical bytes, not upload volume. GB is decimal.

Live storage is the currently published version's files plus SQL data and indexes. It has no usage charge. Both classes share the Free storage pool in the usage table above.

Each logical byte belongs to one class. Internal replicas and recovery copies do not add customer charges. Files downloaded to your own computer do not become retained Spacefast storage. If you keep backup archives in Spacefast, their average retained size counts as files and media.

## Scheduled jobs and backups

Scheduling has no separate fee. Runs use ordinary CPU, invocation, SQL and Workers meters as applicable. Email and saved output use their existing meters. Scheduled response bodies retained on the server add no delivery charge; a manual HTTPS request has its own delivery path.

Managed recovery copies have no separate customer charge.

## Read current usage

Open your team's **Settings → Usage** to see usage against each plan limit. Features outside your plan show **Not included in this plan**.

**Settings → Billing** carries a shorter set of meters next to the plan card: Storage, Team members, Custom domains, and Publishes today. Each one highlights when you reach 80% of the limit.

Two endpoints return the numbers those pages draw. `GET /v1/teams/{teamId}/usage` gives team counters plus a per-space storage breakdown. `GET /v1/spaces/{spaceId}/usage` gives one space's measured period.

## What gets metered

### Current team counters

| Meter | Field | What it counts | At the limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spaces | `spaces` | Live spaces in the team | Blocks on Free. `409` `space_quota_exceeded` |
| Custom domains | `domains` | External domain groups against the plan quota | Blocks. `409` `domain_quota_exceeded` |
| Team members | `members` | Billable seats, live members plus pending invitations | Blocks on Free. `409` `team_member_quota_exceeded` |
| Storage | `storageBytes` | Disk your spaces occupy | Displayed only. Nothing blocks |
| Publishes | `deploysToday` | Publishes since the UTC day started, your pushes and CI builds pooled | Warns only. Publishing continues |

The response also carries `storageQuotaBytes`, `storageOverageBytes`, `deploysDaily`, and `meterStartedAt` so you can compute headroom and know when the metering day rolled over.

### Current per-space measurements

`GET /v1/spaces/{spaceId}/usage` returns one settled period.

| Field | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| `storageBytes` | Average bytes on disk during the period, not the size of what you uploaded |
| `computeSeconds` | Compute the space consumed |
| `bandwidthBytes` | Bytes the space served |
| `emailCount` | Emails the space sent |
| `functionInvocations` | Function calls, counted where the space is known |
| `functionErrors` | Those calls that ended in an error |

These measurements are usage counters, not invoice line items.

## Measurement timing

Storage is measured by the infrastructure that serves your sites, and it lags the present by about an hour. `storageCalculatedAt` tells you how far back the weakest measurement behind the total reaches.

Nothing is deduplicated. A file present in two spaces sits on two disks and counts twice in your team total.

The newest measurement period is always dropped, because partial ingestion underreports it. When the measuring infrastructure is unreachable, usage comes back as `null` rather than a zero or a cached guess. A blank meter means unmeasured, not empty.

Function invocation counts are pushed after each request finishes, so they can arrive late.
