## What every move has in common

1. **Publish the site.** Publish the built output, or hand Spacefast the source and let it build. See [Frameworks and builds](/frameworks).
2. **Check what was flagged.** Platform features Spacefast cannot convert are flagged at publish. Move them to a supported equivalent, or pass `--allow-unsupported-platform-features` to publish without them.
3. **Choose who can open it.** A team's new Spaces start private unless the team changed its default. Make the Space public or create a share link with [Spacefast Share](/share).
4. **Point your domain.** Add the domain to the Space and add the DNS records it asks for. Spacefast issues the certificate once DNS points at it. See [Custom domains](/domains). Keep the old site up until the new address answers.
