---
title: Domain verification records
description: Publish the exact TXT records needed to prove control of a Space hostname.
---

Most hostnames need no verification record: the address records are enough. A TXT record appears only when the hostname is already in use elsewhere. Copy the exact record names and values from your domain's setup instructions.

## Get the records for your hostname

```bash
sf domains add example.com --space my-site
sf domains diagnostics example.com --space my-site
```

The dashboard's domain setup also shows the required records. Add them wherever your DNS is managed. For the complete connection flow, see [Custom domains](/domains).

There are two kinds, and they prove different claims. A WP Cloud record (`atomic-domain-…`) proves the hostname to WP Cloud when another host holds it. A `_spacefast-claim.<domain>` record moves a hostname away from another Space. Do not substitute one for the other or build a TXT value from an example.

## When WP Cloud verification is required

A hostname already used by another WP Cloud client can require an extra TXT record before it can attach to Spacefast. Spacefast retrieves that proof and includes it in the DNS instructions.

The upstream value starts with `atomic-domain-` and belongs at the exact hostname being verified. The apex `example.com` and `www.example.com` can require separate values.

Publish only the currently required WP Cloud verification value at each hostname. Multiple distinct TXT values starting with `atomic-domain-` at the same hostname can prevent verification, even when one matches Spacefast's instructions. Remove a superseded WP Cloud proof when moving the hostname. Keep unrelated TXT records, including email authentication records.

A CNAME cannot coexist with the required TXT record at the same hostname. Follow Spacefast's address-record instructions instead.

## Check and retain the proof

```bash
sf domains check example.com --space my-site
sf domains diagnostics example.com --space my-site
```

DNS propagation can delay a successful check. Inspect every hostname in diagnostics; a verified apex does not prove that `www` is ready.

Keep required verification records after setup so future checks can still prove control. A domain's verification state, routing readiness, and [TLS certificate](/infrastructure/tls-certificates) state are separate. A TXT record alone does not make the hostname live.
