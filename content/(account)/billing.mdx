Manage your plan here. Prices, allowances, and limits for every plan are on [Pricing and limits](/pricing).

## Upgrade, downgrade or cancel

Open your team's **Settings → Billing** to change or cancel a plan. Only owners and admins can change billing. Choose **Open billing portal** for invoices and payment methods.

Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated. Downgrades apply at renewal. Cancellation stops renewal, with access continuing until the paid period ends. There are no refunds. Review the price and effective date before confirming.

## Read the plan through the API

[`GET /v1/teams/{teamId}/entitlements`](/api/reference/teams/getteamentitlements) and [`GET /v1/teams/{teamId}/plan-policy`](/api/reference/teams/getteamplanpolicy) return the resolved `planCode` and active limits. Public plan codes are stable: `free` (Personal), `go` (Go), `plus` (Pro), and `enterprise` (Enterprise).

Use the returned entitlements for feature checks. Read subscription and payment status with [`GET /v1/teams/{teamId}/billing`](/api/reference/teams/getteambilling).

## When a payment fails

Your Spaces keep serving through a grace period, but you cannot change anything.

| Stage | How long | What visitors get | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Past due | 7 days from the failed payment | Spaces serve normally | Reads work. Every write returns `402` [`tenant_past_due`](/errors/tenant_past_due) |
| Suspended | After the grace period ends | Every Space serves `402` with a **This space is paused** page | Reads work. Every write returns `403` [`tenant_suspended`](/errors/tenant_suspended) |

Settle the payment in the billing portal to restore access. API reads remain available in either state.

## Billing is dashboard-only

Plans, checkout, and cancellation live in the dashboard. There is no `sf` command and no API endpoint for changing a plan. API keys and agent credentials cannot make those changes.

Agents can read [plan entitlements](#read-the-plan-through-the-api) and [usage](/usage) through the API.
