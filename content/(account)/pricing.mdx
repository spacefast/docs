## Space pricing and limits

Set per [Space](/spaces). Every plan shares the same **24 MiB** uncompressed compiled Functions bundle limit, covering compiled JavaScript, embedded modules, and their manifest.

<PricingTable table="space" />

### Per-Space Boost

Add Boost to one Space on **Go** or **Pro** for **$9 a month** instead of upgrading the whole team. Boost rides on your team's plan subscription, billing on the same invoice and prorating when you turn it on. Canceling credits unused time on the next invoice, and Boost ends when the plan ends (or without proration if you delete or transfer the Space out).

On a Go or Pro Space, Boost raises:

- **Scheduled crons**: at least **30** basic crons (hourly, daily, or weekly) and **10** five-field crontab crons, up to the config cap. See [Crons](/crons).
- **Transactional email**: adds **5,000** recipients per day on top of the plan's base allowance (Go: 5,200; Pro: 7,000). See [Transactional email](/email).

## At the limit

| When | Pauses | Keeps working |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Personal uses its $1 | New [builds](/ci) and [Workers](/functions) | Deployed Spaces: serving, compute, email |
| You reach a budget you set | New builds and Workers. Nothing above the budget is charged | Deployed Spaces |
| Usage reaches twice that budget (default serving-pause threshold) | Serving too. Visitors see "This space is paused" | Your content and settings. Raise the budget or wait for the month to reset, and serving returns |
| A Space hits one of its limits | That request gets a 429 ([rate limiting](/infrastructure/rate-limiting)), or that publish gets an [error](/errors) naming the limit | The rest of the Space |

## Billing questions

### How am I billed?

Monthly, in US dollars, before tax. Plans and Boost bill at the start of the month; usage past what's included bills at the end. Manage it in [Plans and billing](/billing).

### What if I upgrade or downgrade?

Upgrades apply right away and are prorated. Downgrades apply at renewal; anything over the new limits keeps serving but becomes read-only until it's back under.

### Can prices change?

We may change our fees going forward, in line with the [Terms of Service](https://automattic.com/tos/). If the price of a plan you already pay for goes up, you keep your current price for at least 12 months and get at least 60 days' notice. If you don't agree, you can cancel. Price cuts apply to everyone.

### Can I cap my spending?

Yes. Set a monthly usage budget for a team, a subteam, or a Space in your team's **Settings**, or with [`sf budget set`](/cli/reference#sf-budget-set-amount). Go starts with a $25 default monthly budget and Pro starts with $100 (counted before the plan's included credit) until you set your own. Enterprise has no default budget unless you set one.

Owners and admins get email and dashboard alerts at 50%, 80%, and 100% by default. At 100% of the budget, new builds and Workers pause; at 200% (adjustable between 100% and 200%), serving pauses too. You're never charged above the budget, even if usage briefly runs past it.

### Do you have nonprofit or education pricing?

Yes. Contact sales.
