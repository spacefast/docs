Spacefast for Platforms is for web hosts, domain registrars, agencies, and any organization that runs a lot of sites. You put Spacefast infrastructure inside your own product. Customers see your brand, your prices, and your dashboard, and you don't run any servers.

It's part of WP Cloud (the infrastructure that runs WordPress.com, Pressable, and more). Web hosts already use WP Cloud to sell WordPress under their own names. The [Partner API](/platforms/partner-api) adds the sites and apps your customers build with agents.

## Who it's for

| You are | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| A web host | Sites and apps for your customers, without running servers or building a control panel |
| A domain registrar | A live site for each new domain, right at registration |
| An agency or reseller | One integration for every client site, billed and branded as yours |
| An organization with many sites | A site for each team, created from code, all under one account |

## What you control

- **Brand.** Set your name, logo, support links, and error docs once. When customers claim a site or get access to one, they land on your portal.
- **Customers.** Each customer is an ID from your own system. Spacefast creates the customer the first time you use that ID, so there's no separate signup.
- **Pricing.** Define plans with their own features and quotas. Read each customer's usage records and bill them however you like.
- **Domains.** Serve customer sites from your own hostname, and put the API behind your own origin.
- **Interface.** Everything is API-first. Build your own interface, or give customers scoped tokens so their apps call Spacefast directly.

## What Spacefast handles

Publishing, builds, versions and rollbacks, custom domains and certificates, and the CDN and cache. Spacefast serves your customers' sites the same way it serves every other Space.

## How integration works

1. **Get partner access.** Your account gets a live tenant and a linked test tenant. Provisioning on the test tenant is simulated, and its usage costs nothing.
2. **Create customer Spaces.** Publish for a customer with a tenant API key and the customer's ID. See the [Partner API quickstart](/platforms/partner-api).
3. **Configure your account.** Branding, signing keys, plans, event subscriptions, and hostnames all live in one [system Space](/platforms/partner-api/configuration).
4. **Give customers access.** Issue [customer JWTs](/platforms/partner-api/tokens), each scoped to one customer.
5. **Go live.** Try the failure cases in test mode, then [promote your configuration](/platforms/partner-api/go-live) to the live tenant.
