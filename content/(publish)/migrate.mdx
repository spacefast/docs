Spacefast has a [website importer](https://spacefast.com/migrate/). Give it a public website URL to copy the site's pages to Spacefast. Your original site stays where it is.

## Import from a URL

1. Open [Migrate your website](https://spacefast.com/migrate/).
2. Paste the address of the public website you want to copy.
3. Preview the result.
4. Claim the site to keep it. See [Anonymous publishing and claiming](/anonymous-and-claim).

Review the copy before using it in place of your original site. To use your own domain, follow [Custom domains](/domains).

Have the project files instead of a public URL? [Publish the folder](/publish) or [connect a Git repository](/git).
