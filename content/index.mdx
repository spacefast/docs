Spacefast is the build companion and infrastructure for you and your AI. Connect Spacefast before you start so your agent gets the skills, recipes, database, storage, Workers, auth, email, and domains it needs in one Space—fewer decisions, fewer mistakes, and no stitching five separate services together.

Publish with your AI, drag a folder onto [drop.fast](https://drop.fast) (no install, no account required; see [Drop](/publish#publish)), push to `push.fast`, or run the CLI. Already have a website? Paste its address into the [website importer](https://spacefast.com/migrate/) to copy it into a Space; see [Copy a website](/guides/migrate).

## Start in seconds

### With your AI

Paste this prompt into Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, or any AI that can fetch URLs and make HTTP requests:

<Prompt description="Paste into your AI to connect Spacefast, load skills, and start building." actions={["copy"]}>
Fetch https://spacefast.com/setup.md and follow it.
</Prompt>

Your AI connects to Spacefast, loads Spacefast skills, and builds and publishes directly to your Space. See [Agents](/agents) for plugins, connectors, [MCP](/agents/mcp-server), and [Poppy](/agents/poppy).

### From your terminal

Scaffold a Space or publish a folder directly with `npx`:

```bash
npx -y spacefast publish ./dist
```

No account or global install is required for your first publish. Spacefast creates a Space, uploads changed files, and prints a live URL plus a claim link. Sign in later to keep it, or install the `sf` CLI globally with `npm install -g spacefast`. See [`sf publish`](/cli/reference#sf-publish-dir) and [`sf init`](/cli/reference#sf-init-name).

### From Git or a browser

Push a repository to `push.fast` with [Publish from Git](/git), or drag a folder, a `.zip`, or an `index.html` onto [drop.fast](https://drop.fast).

## How one Space works

A **Space** is your project's home on the web. Every publish that changes files creates an immutable **version** with its own URL. The `live` channel points at the version visitors see, and stays empty before the first publish. Rolling back restores a retained, ready version without rebuilding (by default refreshing current rewrites into a new version, or keeping the exact saved snapshot with `--no-refresh-rewrites`; see [Versions](/versions)). Preview publishes and manual promotion policies leave `live` untouched. For every core term, see the [glossary](/glossary).

Every Space starts static and grows in place without switching products. Build full-stack SpacePress apps with [Collections and CMS](/collections), [Actions, queries, and jobs](/actions), [Database and ORM](/database), and [WordPress and Content API](/wordpress), or combine them with [Workers](/functions), [Transactional email](/email), [Object storage](/object-storage), [Crons](/crons), [Environment variables](/environment-variables), and [Connectors](/connectors) in `spacefast.config.ts`. See [Build on a Space](/build) and [Project configuration](/config-file).

Your team owns the Spaces you claim, even when you are the only member. On [Pro](/pricing), invite teammates to publish and roll back without sharing your login; Personal and Go include one seat. See [Teams and members](/teams).
